# 30 Video Ideas — Sarah's Pilates Channel

Format key: **SHORT** = Reel / Short / TikTok (60–90 sec) | **LONG** = YouTube (8–20 min)

---

## Pilates Form & Education

**1. SHORT — "The pelvic floor cue nobody explains properly"**
Hook: "Every Pilates teacher says 'engage your pelvic floor.' Nobody tells you what that actually feels like."
Body: Sarah demonstrates the difference between gripping, bearing down, and the right gentle lift. Uses plain English, no jargon.
CTA: "If this was news to you, save it. Share it with a friend who does Pilates."

---

**2. LONG — "Pilates for beginners: your first 20 minutes"**
Hook: "You've been putting this off because you think you'll do it wrong. You won't. Let's go."
Body: Full beginner mat session. She talks through every move, explains WHY, adds modifications. Filmed in her living room.
CTA: "Subscribe and I'll send you the 5-minute mobility routine that goes with this."

---

**3. SHORT — "Why your lower back hurts (and the 3-minute fix)"**
Hook: "Tight hips are usually the reason. Here's the test."
Body: Hip flexor tightness test → 3 Pilates-based mobility moves.
CTA: "Try this for a week. Tell me how it goes."

**4. SHORT — "The breathing mistake everyone makes in Pilates"**
Hook: "If you're holding your breath, you're working harder than you need to."
Body: Demonstrates thoracic breathing vs. belly breathing in Pilates context. Shows how proper breath activates the deep core.
CTA: "This one change will make everything click."

---

**5. LONG — "Pilates for back pain: the 30-minute session I'd give my mum"**
Hook: "My mum has had a bad back for 20 years. Here's what I'd actually put her through."
Body: Gentle, evidence-based session. Emphasis on breath, mobilising the spine, avoiding anything that loads the lumbar before stabilisation. Clear medical caveat at start.
CTA: "This isn't a diagnosis. But it IS a place to start."

**6. SHORT — "One move for better posture. Do it in your chair."**
Hook: "You don't need a mat for this. You don't even need to stand up."

CTA: "Set an alarm. Do this every hour. Thank me in a week."

**7. LONG — "What APPI Pilates actually is (and why I chose it)"**

CTA: "If you're thinking about training, I'll be sharing more of the journey."

**8. SHORT — "The hip hinge. The move everyone gets wrong."**
