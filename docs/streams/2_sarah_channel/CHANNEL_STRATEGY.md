# Channel Strategy — Sarah Jane Jones Pilates

---

## Name Options

### Option 1: **Grounded & Moving**
Tagline: *Pilates for real life. From a mum, on the road.*

Why it works: "Grounded" is both a Pilates cue (feet into the floor, breathe, find your centre) and an emotional tone — not flakey, not toxic-positive. "Moving" is literal (exercise) and metaphorical (life in motion, the truck, the expedition). It's warm without being twee.

---

### Option 2: **The Pilates Mum**
Tagline: *Strong core. Wild life. No filters.*

Why it works: Brutally simple. Searchable on day one. "The Pilates Mum" is exactly what people type when they want what Sarah offers. The tagline does the tonal work — it says she's not another polished studio channel.

---

### Option 3: **Core & Go**
Tagline: *Pilates wherever life takes you.*

Why it works: Works as a play on "ready to go" and "core" (Pilates fundamentals). Light, punchy, works as a handle across all platforms. Especially strong when the expedition starts — it becomes a literal description.

---

**Recommended pick:** Option 1 — *Grounded & Moving* — for long-term brand depth. Option 2 is stronger for early SEO.

---

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: The Body
APPI-based Pilates instruction, taught in plain English. Form correction. Real modification for real bodies. Postnatal recovery. Back pain. Mobility for desk workers. Not aspirational fitness — functional strength that makes your day easier.

*Tone: warm teacher, not influencer. "Let's talk about your pelvic floor" not "today's sculpting session."*

### Pillar 2: The Life
Northern mum, two boys, planning to drive a truck around the world. The chaos is the content. Filming Pilates in a car park. Doing mat work on a campsite. The honest version of wellness when you have a full, loud, busy life.

*Tone: funny, honest, self-deprecating without being self-deprecating. Laugh with her.*

### Pillar 3: The Road
The expedition prep, departure, and what Pilates looks like in 40+ countries. Wellness on long drives. Mobility for the kids in the back seat. Practising alongside women in different countries. The 6×6 truck as a studio.

*Tone: adventure with anchors. This is where the channel becomes one-of-a-kind.*

---

## Audience Profile

**Primary:** Women 28–45. Mums. Experiencing some version of: pelvic floor issues, back pain, postnatal body, desk job, not enough time, not enough money for a studio, felt intimidated by Pilates before.

**Secondary:** People curious about long-haul overland travel / slow living who've never thought about Pilates before. They follow for the expedition and stay for the movement.

**Geography (Phase 1):** UK-first. Northern England especially — they'll feel the accent as "one of us." Expands globally from departure.

**They don't want:** Instagram-perfect bodies. Expensive equipment. To be called "babe." Corporate wellness speak. Hustle culture fitness content.

**They do want:** Someone who gets it. Modifications. Honesty. Laughs. Real results explained in real words.

## Growth Targets: Months 1–6

| Month | Goal | Key Actions |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | 200 followers (IG), 50 YouTube subs | Post 3×/wk IG, 1×/wk YT. Focus on searchable content. Announce channel to warm network. |
| 2 | 500 IG, 150 YT | First lead magnet live. Start email list. Reels targeting postnatal/back pain keywords. |
| 3 | 1,000 IG, 400 YT | Collab with 1–2 other UK Pilates/wellness accounts. Expedition story starts (packing, prep). |
| 4 | 2,000 IG, 800 YT | Behind-the-scenes APPI training content. First "Pilates + expedition" crossover videos. |
| 5 | 3,500 IG, 1,500 YT | Qualification month — "I'm nearly there" content. Build anticipation. |
| 6 | 5,000 IG, 2,500 YT | Qualification announcement. Channel mature enough to monetise. Email list 500+. |

These are conservative. Honest content in an undersaturated niche (overland + Pilates + real-mum) can spike faster.

## Monetisation Path (Post-Qualification, Summer 2026+)

**Immediate (Month 7–9):**
- Digital products: paid programmes (4-week postnatal, 6-week back pain, 8-week beginners)
- Priced £15–£35. Simple PDF + video links to unlisted YT.

**Medium-term (Month 10–18):**
- Online classes: live Zoom (£10/class) or membership access (£15–20/month)
- Email list becomes primary sales channel — not algorithm-dependent
- Small brand partnerships: mat brands, wellness supplements, travel gear (only stuff she'd actually use on the truck)

**Long-term (Expedition Phase):**
- "Pilates on the Road" membership: follow-along classes filmed in each country
- Documentary-style YouTube series (ad revenue + Patreon)
- Speaking / retreat opportunities
- APPI instructor training referrals

**What to avoid:** Selling before qualified. Promising therapeutic outcomes on social before the credential is in hand. Rushing to ads before the audience trusts her.
