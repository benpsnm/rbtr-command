## Three Things to Do This Week

1. **Pick the channel name.** Grounded & Moving or The Pilates Mum — make the call and register the handles on every platform now, even if you're not posting yet. Handles go fast.

2. **Record Video 1.** The beginner session script is here. Living room, tripod, morning light. One take-and-edit. It will not be perfect. Post it anyway. Perfect content posted in six months beats imperfect content posted never.

3. **Set up the email list.** Mailchimp or Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — free tier is fine to start. Create the welcome sequence and attach the 5-Minute Morning Mobility lead magnet. Put the link in the IG bio. Every person who gives you their email is 100x more valuable than a follower.

---

## What Success Looks Like

By qualification day (summer 2026): 5,000 Instagram followers. 2,500 YouTube subscribers. An email list of 500+. A channel that has been running for 12+ weeks and has its rhythm.

By departure day: A channel that is mature, trusted, and ready to become a travel-Pilates documentary series. An audience that feels they know Sarah and is waiting to go with her.

The channel doesn't launch when Sarah qualifies. It matures by then. That's the whole game.
