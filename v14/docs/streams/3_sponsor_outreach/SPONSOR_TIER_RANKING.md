## How Tiers Are Assigned

Each sponsor scored on three axes:
- **£ value** — raw ask value from build spec
- **Brand fit** — how naturally an Arocs 6x6 family expedition aligns with their marketing
- **Likelihood of yes** — expedition history, social-first culture, UK presence, accessibility of DM

4 custom tiers (not the DB tiers 1/2/3 which are approach-month based):

| Tier | Label | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| A | Anchor Partners | £5,000+ ask + high fit + reachable |
| B | Strong Partners | £2,000–£4,999 + strong fit |
| C | Gear Partners | £500–£1,999 + clear expedition role |
| D | Supporting / Speculative | Sub-£500, media, audience plays |
