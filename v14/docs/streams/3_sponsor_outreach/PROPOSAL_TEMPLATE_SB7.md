## How to Use This Template

The StoryBrand framework puts the SPONSOR as the hero, not RBTR. Most expedition proposals fail because they make the expedition the star. This template reverses that. The sponsor solves a problem by partnering with RBTR. Your job is to help them see how their brand grows by being on this truck.

Each section is 2–4 sentences when filled in. Total proposal: 500–800 words + visuals.
