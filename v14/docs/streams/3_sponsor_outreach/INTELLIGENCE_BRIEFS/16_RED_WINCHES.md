## Risk Flags

- **Winch capacity for 33 tonnes.** Discuss the realistic load scenario with RED Winches engineers. A winch assist (using the truck's own momentum + winch) is different from a dead-pull recovery. They need to spec the right product.
- **Synthetic vs steel rope.** Strongly recommend synthetic for an expedition — lighter, safer if it breaks, kinder to the winch drum. RED Winches may have a preference.
