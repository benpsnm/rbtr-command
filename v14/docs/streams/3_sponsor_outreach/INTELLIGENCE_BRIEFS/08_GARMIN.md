## Company Background

**Garmin Ltd** — US technology company founded 1989. Headquartered in Olathe, Kansas (and Schaffhausen, Switzerland for tax purposes). Listed on NASDAQ.

Relevant products: inReach Mini 2 (satellite communicator + GPS + SOS), inReach Messenger, GPSMAP handhelds, aviation products.
