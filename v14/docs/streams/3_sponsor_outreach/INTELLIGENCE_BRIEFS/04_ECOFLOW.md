2. **Delta Pro Ultra in an actual expedition context** — This is their premium product. Showing it working in the Sahara, at altitude in the Himalayas, and in Central Asia is marketing content they can't manufacture.
3. **Family audience crossover** — EcoFlow is targeting the family outdoor market. RBTR's family angle (two young boys, Sarah's wellness content) aligns.
4. **UK-to-worldwide journey** — EcoFlow is building UK market share. A UK family going global on EcoFlow power is a UK success story they can use domestically.
5. **Content volume** — 400+ videos mentioning EcoFlow over 45 months. That's an enormous content library.
