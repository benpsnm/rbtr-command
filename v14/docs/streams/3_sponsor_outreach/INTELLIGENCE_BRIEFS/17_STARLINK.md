## Company Background

**Starlink** — Satellite internet service operated by SpaceX. Headquartered in Hawthorne, California, USA.

Products relevant to RBTR:
- **Starlink Standard** — The standard flat panel hardware
- **Starlink Mini** — Compact, portable unit, designed for mobile use
- **Starlink Global Roaming** — Subscription plan enabling use in any covered country

**Coverage (as of 2025):** Global coverage including most of Europe, Central Asia, India/Nepal, East Africa, Southern Africa, SE Asia, Australia. Coverage in specific remote areas varies — verify at route-planning time.

## Current Marketing Themes (2025–2026)

- **"Internet from anywhere"** — Core consumer/residential positioning
- **Mobile/RV market** — Starlink has an active RV/mobile tier
- **Expedition and adventure** — Growing use case in overlanding, sailboats, yachts, remote operations
- **Business critical connectivity** — Remote work from anywhere narrative

## Sponsorship History

Starlink does not have a formal traditional sponsorship program in the way equipment brands do. However:
- They have done partnership announcements with maritime and aviation operators
- They engage with high-profile users who demonstrate their service
- The adventure/expedition use case is one they've highlighted in their own marketing materials
- Overlanding YouTubers have featured Starlink and received product support on a case-by-case basis

## Why RBTR Specifically

1. **The practical case is perfect for them.** A family homeschooling on the road needs internet every day. In Kyrgyzstan. In Ethiopia. In Outback Australia. Starlink's global roaming service is the only product that makes this possible.
2. **Working from the road.** Ben manages PSNM remotely, Sarah teaches the boys — Starlink is the business infrastructure, not a luxury.
3. **Demonstrating global roaming capability.** Showing Starlink working in 30+ countries is a live product demonstration nobody else provides at this scale.
4. **The Pamir Highway.** A Starlink connection on one of the most remote roads on earth is a marketing moment they can't manufacture.
5. **Content creation dependency.** Uploading 4K video from remote locations requires Starlink. The product is literally what makes the content possible.

## Risk Flags

- **Formal partnership may not be accessible.** SpaceX is not a typical sponsor. If the formal route fails, consider: simply paying for Starlink Global Roaming as a business expense (the service is genuinely needed) and creating content about it regardless. The free marketing still happens.
- **Coverage gaps.** Verify Starlink coverage on the route, particularly Pakistan, India, and some African countries before committing to Starlink as the primary connectivity solution.
- **Subscription cost is ongoing.** £75–100/month × 45 months = £3,375–4,500 total. Even partial support is valuable.
