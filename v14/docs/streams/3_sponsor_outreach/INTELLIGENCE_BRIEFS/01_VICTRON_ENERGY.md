## Company Background

**Victron Energy BV** — Headquartered in Almere, Netherlands. Founded 1975. Private company.

Makes inverter/chargers, battery monitors, MPPT solar charge controllers, battery management systems, and related electrical components for off-grid, marine, RV, and backup power applications.

The Quattro 24/5000 (the unit in Ben's spec) is their flagship residential/vehicle inverter-charger — handles 5kW continuous output, charges from shore power or generator, manages solar input.

**UK presence:** Strong. UK distributor network. Key distributors include Bimble Solar (Manchester), Merlin Equipment, and others. Victron has a UK technical support presence.

**Revenue (est.):** Private, but a substantial European power electronics company. Not a startup.

## Current Marketing Themes (2025–2026)

- **DIY off-grid and van conversion community** — Victron has a massive YouTube presence through third-party build channels. They don't need to advertise — their products are recommended organically by builders.
- **LiFePO4 transition** — Victron's BMS and battery monitor products are essential in lithium conversions. They're riding the LiFePO4 wave hard.
- **Remote monitoring / Victron Remote Management (VRM)** — Their cloud platform showing real-time battery and inverter data. Significant marketing push.
- **Cerbo GX + Touch 50 screen** — Their data hub and touchscreen display. A visual centrepiece in any build.

Examples (verify these — public knowledge at time of writing):

## Why RBTR Specifically

Victron's typical placement is campervans, sailboats, and residential off-grid. A 33-tonne 6x6 expedition truck is categorically different:

1. **Scale of the install** — Quattro 24/5000 + 400Ah LiFePO4 + 1,600W solar. This is a substantial system, not a minimal van build.
2. **Duration** — 45 months continuous use. The longevity data is genuinely valuable to them.
3. **Environments** — From Pamir Highway altitude (-20°C) to African heat (+45°C). Real-world performance envelope beyond anything a van conversion provides.
4. **VRM data** — The VRM portal showing live performance data from the expedition is a marketing asset they could reference publicly.
5. **The story** — A family living off Victron power for 45 months. Not a weekend adventure. Full-time dependency.
