## Company Background

**Mercedes-Benz Trucks** — Commercial vehicle division of Daimler Truck AG, listed separately from Mercedes-Benz AG since 2021. One of the world's largest truck manufacturers.

The **Arocs** is Mercedes-Benz Trucks' premium heavy-duty construction/off-road truck range. The 3258 6x6 is the all-wheel-drive variant designed for extreme terrain.

**UK presence:** Mercedes-Benz Trucks UK Ltd. Dealer network across the UK. UK press/media team. UK marketing department.

**Daimler Truck AG** is listed separately from Mercedes-Benz cars — important for contacts. The commercial truck PR team is not the same as the Mercedes-Benz car press office.

Mercedes-Benz Trucks has a history of expedition and adventure partnerships:
- Historical: Dakar Rally technical support vehicles have used Mercedes trucks

They tend to support expeditions that align with the Arocs's positioning — heavy terrain, extreme conditions. A 33-tonne 6x6 family expedition is exactly that brand territory.

## Why RBTR Specifically

1. **It's their truck.** Not a Land Cruiser. Not a Hilux. Not a Sprinter. The Arocs 3258 6x6 — a genuinely unusual expedition vehicle choice — and the story starts with "why this truck?"
2. **The engineering story.** Ben is a 20-year fabricator building on an Arocs because of its engineering. That's an authentic endorsement that their marketing team can't manufacture.
3. **Departure day.** The departure from Rotherham on 1 July 2027 is a public event. An Arocs rolling out of South Yorkshire is a UK story.
4. **Route extremes.** The Arocs proving itself on the Pamir Highway (4,655m), African safari terrain, Central Asian mud roads — content their construction/fleet marketing team normally doesn't have.
5. **The scale.** 45 countries. A family. 33 tonnes. This is not a weekend trip in a high-roof van.

## The Ask

**Value:** ~£5,000 (in kind / technical support, not necessarily cash)

Potential support forms:
- Technical access: factory/engineering visit, workshop support at MB Trucks UK dealer during build
- Parts: genuine MB parts at cost (filters, service items for 45-month expedition)
- Branding: permission to use MB Trucks UK logo in RBTR marketing (in exchange for feature content)
- Co-branding: MB Trucks UK press release about the RBTR expedition
- Cash: unlikely for a corporation of this size, but not impossible for a co-branded campaign

**Realistic ask:** Technical support + parts at cost + press release = £3,000–5,000 equivalent value.

## Risk Flags

- **Corporate process is slow.** MB Trucks UK will need internal approvals for any partnership. Start this conversation 9–12 months before departure (summer 2026 at latest).
- **Marketing vs technical department.** The pitch needs to land with marketing, not the truck sales team. Dealers are sales people — they'll need guidance on how to route the intro.
- **Exclusivity not required.** Ben's not asking MB Trucks to be the exclusive truck partner — they're already the truck. There's no competition.
- **The truck already exists** — if they don't provide any support, the truck is still an Arocs. Don't over-negotiate.
