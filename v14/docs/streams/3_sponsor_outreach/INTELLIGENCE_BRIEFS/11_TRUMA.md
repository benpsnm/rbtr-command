## Company Background

**Truma Gerätetechnik GmbH & Co. KG** — German manufacturer. Founded 1949. Family-owned. Based in Munich area.

Key products: Combi heater/water heater systems, VarioHeat gas heater, AquaGo instant hot water, LevelControl (automated levelling), digital control units.

**UK presence:** Truma UK Ltd. UK dealer network through motorhome dealers and accessory shops. UK press/marketing team.

**Market:** Motorhomes, caravans, camper vans. Growing into expedition vehicles.

## Why RBTR Specifically

1. **Combi heater in a true expedition vehicle.** Truma's products are usually in comfortable motorhomes. An expedition truck in the Kazakh steppe in November is a different beast entirely. That performance data is genuinely valuable.
2. **Year-round use, extreme temperatures.** From UK autumn cold to Central Asian winter to tropical humidity — the Combi heater covering all of this is their credibility case.
3. **Moving into the expedition market.** Truma has been pushing beyond the motorhome OEM market. RBTR is a showcase for that expansion.
4. **UK family story.** Truma UK gets a UK product in a UK story.
