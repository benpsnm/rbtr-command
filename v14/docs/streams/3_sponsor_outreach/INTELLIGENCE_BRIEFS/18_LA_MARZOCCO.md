## Risk Flags

- **Power consumption.** At 1,100W, the Linea Mini needs careful management in the truck's electrical system. This is solvable but must be planned from the start.
- **Water quality.** The Linea Mini requires filtered water. The truck's Berkey or Berkefeld filtration system handles this — but hard water in some countries can damage the boiler over time. A water softener cartridge may be needed.
- **Vibration and transport.** Espresso machines are not designed for corrugated road vibration. Mounting needs careful thought to protect the boiler and group head. La Marzocco's engineering team may want to advise on this.
- **This pitch needs the truck to look real.** La Marzocco is a premium brand. They will want to see that the truck is genuinely impressive before associating their brand with it. Don't approach until you have striking truck photos.
