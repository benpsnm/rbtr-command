## Company Background

**Compagnie Générale des Établissements Michelin SCA** — French multinational. Founded 1889. One of the world's largest tyre manufacturers. Publicly listed.

The **XZL 365/80 R20** is Michelin's military/expedition tyre — used on NATO military vehicles, UN trucks, and serious expedition builds. It is not a consumer tyre. It is purpose-built for heavy vehicles on any terrain.

**UK presence:** Michelin UK Ltd, based in Stoke-on-Trent (historically). UK commercial vehicle division handles fleet and specialist tyre supply. UK press office handles media/PR.

## Sponsorship History

Michelin has a documented history of expedition sponsorship:
- **Camel Trophy** — The Camel Trophy off-road events of the 1980s and 1990s used Michelin tyres extensively
- **Overland and rally expeditions** — Michelin has worked with expedition teams at the Dakar Rally and Trans-Siberian expeditions
- **Michelin Challenge Bibendum** — Their sustainability driving events with various vehicle types

In the UK, Michelin has supported adventure campaigns through their truck/commercial division more than their consumer division. The XZL tyre specifically has a heritage in serious vehicle builds.
