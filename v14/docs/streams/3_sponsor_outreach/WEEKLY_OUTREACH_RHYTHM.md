# RBTR Sponsor Outreach — Weekly Rhythm
# Sustainable pacing for a solo founder also running PSNM + building a truck
# Generated: 2026-05-04

---

## The Core Principle

Outreach is not about volume. It is about consistency. One well-crafted, well-timed touch per week, to the right person, beats five generic emails per day to a spray list.

Ben is one person. He runs PSNM, consults for Eternal Kustoms, manages a house renovation, is physically training, and is building a 33-tonne truck. Outreach that requires 4 hours a day doesn't happen. Outreach that takes 90 minutes per week, in a structured block, gets done.

**Weekly commitment: 90 minutes.** Non-negotiable block. In the calendar. Not negotiable for warehouse calls.

**Don't go faster than this.** More isn't better. Sending 10 emails in week 1 means 10 follow-up threads in week 2. It compounds. Start slow, build rhythm.

## The Weekly Block — Tuesday Morning

**Tuesday, 7:00am–8:30am**

Reason for Tuesday: Monday is for catching up on the weekend and PSNM operations. Wednesday–Friday are execution days. Tuesday morning is the window when energy is high and the week's tasks are cleared.

**Never do outreach:**
- On a Friday afternoon (emails die over the weekend)
- On a Monday (worst open-rate day for B2B)
- First thing after a bad PSNM day (the energy comes through in the writing)

## The 90-Minute Block Breakdown

### 0:00–0:15 — Review Active Conversations (15 min)

Open the portal Sponsor section. Scan:
- Who replied this week?
- Who is overdue for a follow-up?
- Who should move to the next touch in their sequence?

Update statuses in the portal. Prioritise the day's outreach.

### 0:15–0:45 — Compose New Touches (30 min)

Write 2–3 personalised touches. Could be:
- A Touch 1 first email to a new sponsor
- A Touch 3 follow-up email (with a relevant photo or update)
- A Touch 7 post-podcast mention
- A Touch 9 honest check-in

Quality over speed. Each email gets read once by one person. Make it count.

### 0:45–1:00 — LinkedIn Activity (15 min)

- Send 1–2 connection requests (with the standard note from Touch 2)
- Reply to any pending LinkedIn messages from sponsors
- Leave 2–3 genuine comments on sponsor content

### 1:00–1:20 — Research One New Sponsor (20 min)

Pick the next sponsor from the priority contact order (see SPONSOR_TIER_RANKING.md). Spend 20 minutes:
- Find the right contact name (LinkedIn)
- Read their most recent LinkedIn post or company update
- Find one specific hook for the Touch 1 email (a campaign, product launch, or relevant content)
- Draft the subject line

Don't send yet. Add to the drafts queue for next week's block.

### 1:20–1:30 — Log and Plan (10 min)

Update the sponsor tracker in the portal:
- Mark all touches sent today
- Set next follow-up dates
- Note any intelligence gathered (who they're following, what they're promoting, any events they're attending)

## Triage: When to Keep Going vs When to Stop

**Keep going if:**
- They've opened 2+ emails
- They've accepted a LinkedIn connection
- They commented on or liked your content
- They said "contact me in X months" and the time has passed
- A competitor has expressed interest (genuine pressure to decide)

**Stop at Touch 9 (the honest check-in) if:**
- Zero engagement across 8 previous touches
- No email opens at all
- They're a small company with no identifiable marketing contact

**Immediately stop and switch to nurture-only if:**
- They've said "not now" — leave them alone for 6 months then one email
- They've said "not for us" — one thank-you reply, then done
- They've asked not to be contacted — honour it completely

## Content-Led Outreach (The Parallel Track)

Outreach does not only happen in the 90-minute Tuesday block. Content is outreach. Every time Ben posts a build video and tags a brand in it, that's a touch.

**Create content that gives you a reason to contact sponsors:**
- "This week I started on the electrical system — here's why I went with Victron"
- "The slide-out mechanism — Expedition Meister, this is your product in action"
- "Day 1 of the roof build — looking at Alu-Cab options"

This generates organic outreach loops without using the 90-minute block.

---

## Energy Management

Outreach drains energy. Cold rejection (no reply) is demoralising if you let it be. Rules:

1. **No reply is not rejection.** It's just noise. They don't know you yet.
2. **A no is information.** It closes a loop and frees up mental space.
3. **Do not negotiate with silence.** You send the sequence. They respond or they don't. You move on.
4. **Wins are fuel.** When a sponsor replies positively, log it in the portal and let it motivate the next 10 attempts.

## Quarterly Review

Every 3 months, Ben sits down for one hour and asks:
- How many sponsors are now confirmed vs 3 months ago?
- Which approach worked best (email, LinkedIn, content mention)?
- Which sponsor categories are most responsive?
- What's changed in the build that creates new outreach hooks?
- Who in my network can I ask to make a warm intro I haven't asked yet?

Update the priority contact order based on answers.
