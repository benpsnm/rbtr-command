# RBTR Sponsor Outreach — 16-Touch Nurture Sequence
# For sponsors who don't reply to Touch 1 (first email)
# Applies to all Tier A and B sponsors. Adapt for Tier C.
# Generated: 2026-05-04

---

## Core Principles

1. **Never aggressive.** Never chase. Always adding value or creating relevance.
2. **Each touch must have a reason to exist.** Not "just following up" — give them something.
3. **Channel variation matters.** Email → LinkedIn → Organic mention → Email → Real-world interaction.
4. **Respect silence.** If at Touch 10 there's still nothing, the door is quietly left open.
5. **Patience is the strategy.** Sponsors are not chased — they're made curious.

### Touch 5 — Organic Comment on Their Content
**Channel:** LinkedIn / Instagram comment
**Timing:** Day 20
**Script:** Leave a genuine, specific comment on a piece of their content. Not about RBTR. Just engaging as a person.

Example: *On a Victron "solar setup" video: "Solid setup — ran 4 × 400W bifacial on my last build, that MPPT SmartSolar is hard to fault."*

**Success criteria:** They see the name. Brand recall without a pitch.

### Touch 10 — Industry Event / Show
**Channel:** In person (if applicable)
**Timing:** Next relevant trade show — Overland Event, Caravan & Motorhome Show, DSEI, etc.
**Script:** Introduce yourself at their stand. Not as a supplicant — as a builder showing what you're doing. Have the truck photos on your phone.

> "I'm Ben Greenwood — I've been emailing [contact name]. Building a 33-tonne Arocs for a 45-country family expedition. I've specced your [product] in. Just wanted to put a face to the name."

**Success criteria:** A handshake, a card, an intro to the right person

## When to Skip Touches

- **If they say "contact us in X months"** — honour it. Skip to the relevant touch near that date.
- **If they engage positively at any touch** — stop the sequence. Open a proposal conversation.
- **If they say no** — thank them, close the sequence, note the reason.
- **If a competitor lands the category** — decide: keep this brand for a second category, or close cleanly.

---

## The "Already Said No" Recovery

If a brand said no in a previous conversation, wait 6 months. Then:

> Subject: RBTR — [COMPANY NAME] — 6 months on
>
> [Name],
>
> You passed on RBTR last year — fair enough. We've come a long way since then. [Channel now has X subscribers / truck is now complete / we depart in X months.]
>
> If the situation has changed on your end, I'd welcome another look. If not, no hard feelings.
>
> Ben

One email. No sequence. Respect the previous decision.
