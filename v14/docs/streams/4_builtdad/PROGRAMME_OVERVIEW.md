# Built Dad — Programme Overview

## What This Is

Built Dad started Monday 20 April 2026. Day 1. You didn't announce it. You didn't post about it. You just decided.

That matters. Because the best things you've ever done — leaving PSNM, saying yes to the expedition, marrying Sarah — didn't come with fanfare either. They came from a quiet knowing that something had to change, or be built, or be honoured.

This is you building something before the truck rolls.

---

## Why It Exists

You were raised by your grandparents. Your grandad was the template. Not perfect — no man is — but present, steady, capable, gentle in the right moments and hard when it counted. He showed you that being a man isn't a performance, it's a practice. You watched him. You absorbed him. And then you lost him before you were done learning.

You have two sons now. Hudson is six. Benson is nearly three. They are watching you the same way you watched your grandad. They are absorbing everything — how you handle stress, how you treat Sarah, how you respond to fear, how you get up when something knocks you flat. The expedition is coming. Before you drive that truck into the desert, you need to be the version of yourself that those boys deserve to follow.

That's what Built Dad is. It's not a fitness programme (though you'll be fitter). It's not a therapy programme (though things will come up). It's not a leadership course. It's 56 days of intentional fathering, in private, with no audience except the people who matter most.

---

## The Nate Standard

Your mentor Nate has been in the background of this for a while. You know what he'd say if you asked him directly: *stop preparing to prepare, and just become it.* Built Dad is you taking that advice. Not building towards the man — being the man, one day at a time, for 56 days until it's not a programme anymore, it's just who you are.

---

## The 8-Week Structure

Each week has a theme. The themes build on each other. By Week 8 you're not learning new things — you're consolidating everything into a single clear identity: father, husband, expedition leader, man.

| Week | Days | Theme |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 | 1–7 | **Foundations** — Who you actually are right now. No performance, no spin. |
| 2 | 8–14 | **The Boys** — Hudson and Benson specifically. What they need from you, not generically, but exactly. |
| 3 | 15–21 | **The Marriage** — Sarah sees everything. What does she need to see more of? |
| 4 | 22–28 | **The Body** — Physical readiness for expedition leadership. Cold, tired, hungry, still present. |
| 5 | 29–35 | **The Legacy** — Your grandad. What you inherited, what you're passing on, what you're leaving behind. |
| 6 | 36–42 | **The Fear** — What actually scares you about the expedition. About fatherhood. Say it out loud. |
| 7 | 43–49 | **The Commitment** — Re-choosing everything. Sarah, the boys, the expedition, the man you're building. |
| 8 | 50–56 | **The Hand-Off** — What you're handing to Hudson and Benson before you lead them overland. The end-state. |

---

## The End-State: Day 56

When you finish Day 56, the goal is not that you've completed a programme. The goal is that the programme is no longer necessary because the habits, the reflections, the honesty with Sarah, the presence with the boys — it's all just you now.

Day 56 is not a finish line. It's an evidence date. By then you should be able to look Hudson in the eye and say, without flinching: *I am ready to lead you.*

The expedition starts in July 2027. Built Dad finishes mid-June 2026. You have over a year of being *that man* before the truck rolls. That's the plan.

---

## Notes on Tone

This is a private commitment. It's not content. It's not a story arc for the expedition documentary. If it becomes content later, fine, but that's not why you're doing it.

Nobody needs to know you're doing Built Dad except the people in your house. Sarah can see it in how you show up. Hudson will feel it. Benson will grow into it without knowing its name.

That's enough. That's the point.
