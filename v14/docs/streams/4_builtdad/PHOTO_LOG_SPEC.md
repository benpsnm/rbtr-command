# Built Dad — Photo Log (Days 1–56)

One photo per day. Nothing staged. These build a 56-image essay of a man becoming more intentionally himself. No filters required. Low light is fine. Ugly is fine. The goal is evidence — something Hudson and Benson can look at one day and understand.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations

**Day 1** — Your face. Not posed. Just your face, wherever you are when you decide to take it today. This is the before.
**Day 2** — Your hands doing something. Working, holding something, resting. Your hands are your grandad's hands too.
**Day 3** — Something in your home that you built or fixed. Evidence of capability.
**Day 4** — Sarah, without her knowing. Not a portrait — a moment.
**Day 5** — Where you go when you need to think. The actual place.
**Day 6** — Benson asleep. Your kid, small and safe.
**Day 7** — Your reflection. Mirror, window, water, anything. End of Week 1.

**Day 11** — What you look like after something hard — cold, training, whatever it was today.
**Day 12** — Something that belonged to your grandad, if you have it. Or something that reminds you of him.
