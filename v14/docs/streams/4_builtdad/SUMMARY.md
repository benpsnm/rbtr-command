## Tone and Approach

The material avoids self-help language and generic motivational framing. It's anchored to specifics: PSNM survival, Hudson (6), Benson (nearly 3), Sarah, the grandad template, the Nate influence, the July 2027 expedition. The Daily Prompts mix tender and uncomfortable in deliberate rotation. The Sarah Check-Ins treat her as a partner with clear sight, not a support role.

The running theme: a man doesn't build a programme, he builds himself, in private, until the programme is redundant.

## Portal Integration Summary

The spec calls for:
- Auto-advancing daily prompt card with a simple "mark as reflected" toggle
- Weekly milestone checklist (prompt-style, not compliance-tracked)
- 56-slot photo grid with day-specific prompts, private upload, and Day 56 essay export
- Partner check-in tab with weekly questions and done toggle
- Summary card in `sec-bestself` linking back to the full section
- Full archive post-Day 56, persistent

No gamification, no notifications, no public component. Built Dad in the portal is as private as Built Dad in practice.

## What's Not Here
