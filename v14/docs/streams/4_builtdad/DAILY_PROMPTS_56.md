# Built Dad — Daily Reflection Prompts (Days 1–56)

One prompt per day. Spend 5–10 minutes with it. Write, voice memo, stare at the ceiling — whatever works. The goal is honest contact with the question, not a polished answer.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations (Days 1–7)

**Day 1**
Without editing yourself: who are you right now, as a father? Not who you want to be. Not who you think you should be. Who are you actually, today?

**Day 2**
What did your grandad do as a man that you've never consciously copied but find yourself doing anyway? Name one specific thing.

**Day 3**
What do you want Hudson to say about you when he's thirty? Not "my dad was great" — specific. One sentence, in his voice.

**Day 4**
What's the thing you keep almost saying to Sarah but don't? Not an argument — something real and true that you haven't found the words for yet.

**Day 5**
When PSNM was at its worst, what kept you upright? Was it you? Was it something outside you? Was it fear? Be honest.

**Day 6**
Benson is nearly three. He is watching you more closely than you realise. What has he seen this week that you're glad about? What has he seen that you'd quietly like to take back?

**Day 7**
End of Week 1. What have you been avoiding thinking about since Monday? Name it. Don't solve it. Just name it.

---

## Week 2 — The Boys (Days 8–14)

**Day 8**
Hudson is six. He's forming his template of what a man does under pressure. What have you shown him about that? What do you want to show him differently?

**Day 9**
You are on Day 10 of Built Dad. What has changed, even slightly, since Day 1? If nothing has changed, why not?

**Day 10**
What's one thing Benson does that stops you in your tracks — that makes you catch your breath with something like grief or gratitude? Why does it do that to you?

**Day 11**
If Hudson could hear your internal monologue for one full day, what would he think of his dad? Would it match what he sees on the outside?

**Day 12**
The expedition means months in a truck across rough terrain. Hudson and Benson will be there. What are you most excited about showing them? What fear sits underneath that excitement?

**Day 13**
Think of one thing your grandad did with you when you were small that you've replicated with Hudson or Benson. Exactly. If you haven't done it yet, why not?

**Day 14**
End of Week 2. What did one of the boys teach you this week? Not symbolically. Literally. What did you learn from watching or listening to them?

---

## Week 3 — The Marriage (Days 15–21)

**Day 15**
Sarah has watched you rebuild from PSNM. She's been alongside everything. What has she never asked credit for that you should name — to yourself, right now?

**Day 16**
When you're at your worst as a partner — tired, withdrawn, stuck in your head — what does that look like? Be specific. Not a diagnosis. A description.

**Day 17**
The expedition is also Sarah's choice. She said yes. What has that yes cost her that you don't think about often enough?

**Day 18**
What do you want your marriage to look like on Day 1 of the expedition? Not perfect — real. What would "healthy and ready" look like between you and Sarah by then?

**Day 19**
When did you last make Sarah laugh — really laugh? Not a chuckle. A proper laugh. What was it?

**Day 20**
What's one thing about Sarah that you've stopped noticing because you've been together long enough to take it for granted? Bring it back into focus.

**Day 21**
End of Week 3. Write (or think) one thing Sarah deserves to hear from you that you haven't said recently. You don't have to say it tonight — but you have to know it tonight.

---

## Week 4 — The Body (Days 22–28)

**Day 22**
Expedition leadership is physical. You need to be functional when you're exhausted, cold, and far from comfort. Where is your body right now in that readiness?

**Day 23**
Cold exposure — what do you notice in yourself in those seconds before you go under? Not the philosophy of it. The actual sensation, the resistance, the moment before you choose it.

**Day 24**
What physical habit have you let slip in the last year that you know you need back before the truck rolls? Be honest about what's stopped you.

**Day 25**
You will carry a lot on this expedition — literally. Packs, equipment, responsibility. What are you currently carrying in your body that isn't expedition weight? Tension, old injury, accumulated stress. Where does it live?

**Day 26**
What does your body do when you're proud of yourself? Not the thoughts — the physical sensation. Locate it.

**Day 27**
If you were advising Hudson at 30 on how to maintain his body as a father, what would you tell him? Does your current behaviour match that advice?

**Day 28**
End of Week 4. What's the hardest physical thing you've done this week? Did it make you feel like the man you're building, or did it highlight a gap?

---

## Week 5 — The Legacy (Days 29–35)

**Day 29**
Your grandad died before you were finished learning from him. What question do you most wish you could have asked him?

**Day 30**
What values did you inherit from your grandparents — not explicitly taught, just absorbed — that are now in your parenting without you consciously putting them there?

**Day 31**
What would your grandad think of the expedition? Not what you hope he'd think. What would he actually think — knowing what he knew, being who he was?

**Day 32**
What do you want Hudson and Benson to inherit from you that has nothing to do with the expedition, nothing to do with business, nothing spectacular — just the quiet stuff?

**Day 33**
Is there anything you inherited from your grandparents that you've decided, consciously or not, stops with you? Something you're not passing forward?

**Day 34**
When you think about your grandad's hands, what do you think about? Specifically.

**Day 35**
End of Week 5. Write a one-paragraph letter to your grandad. Tell him what Built Dad is. Tell him why.

---

## Week 6 — The Fear (Days 36–42)

**Day 36**
What is the actual thing you're afraid of on the expedition? Not the surface answer. The one underneath that.

**Day 37**
What are you afraid of as a father that you haven't told anyone — not Sarah, not Nate, nobody?

**Day 38**
PSNM survival left marks. What fear from that period is still active in you, even if it's quieter now?

**Day 39**
Are you afraid Hudson won't like who you are? Be precise — not that he won't love you, but that he won't *like* you, respect you, choose your company. Sit with that.

**Day 40**
What's the version of this expedition that goes wrong? Not the logistics version — the family version. What does failure look like with the people you love?

**Day 41**
What are you afraid of failing at that you have never named as a fear because naming it would make it feel too possible?

**Day 42**
End of Week 6. Which of this week's fears is the most useful — the one that, if you take it seriously, makes you better? What would taking it seriously actually look like?

---

## Week 7 — The Commitment (Days 43–49)

**Day 43**
Re-choose Sarah. Not because you have to, not as a formality — actually choose her. What are you choosing? Be specific about who she is to you now.

**Day 44**
Re-choose Hudson. What is your specific, personal commitment to him as his father? Not a generic parenting promise — a Ben-to-Hudson commitment.

**Day 45**
Re-choose Benson. He's nearly three. He's still becoming. What are you committing to for the years ahead as he figures out who he is?

**Day 46**
Re-choose the expedition. Why is this the right thing for your family, not just for you? If you can't answer that cleanly, what still needs to be resolved?

**Day 47**
What does Built Dad commit you to *after* Day 56? What are you refusing to let slip back once this is done?

**Day 48**
Nate has been a mentor to you. What has he shown you — about manhood, fatherhood, or leading — that you've integrated into yourself? Have you told him?

**Day 49**
End of Week 7. Write down the three things you are most committed to as a man. Not goals. Commitments. The things that, if broken, you'd have to answer for.

---

## Week 8 — The Hand-Off (Days 50–56)

**Day 50**
What are you handing to Hudson before the expedition? Not equipment. What of yourself, deliberately, intentionally?

**Day 51**
What are you handing to Benson before the expedition? He'll be too young to understand it verbally. What will he feel, even if he can't name it?

**Day 52**
What are you handing to Sarah before the expedition, as her partner — not as the expedition leader, not as the dad — as the man she chose?

**Day 53**
If Built Dad worked — if these 56 days did what they were supposed to do — what is different about you now compared to Day 1? Not aspirational. Observable.

**Day 54**
What do you want the boys to see in you on the first morning of the expedition? If they wake up in the truck, look at you, and see *that man* — what does he look like?

**Day 55**
What does your grandad look like in you right now? Where do you feel him most clearly?

**Day 56**
You made it. Not because 56 days is impressive — because you showed up every single day to something private and true and difficult. Say what you need to say to yourself. Then close the programme. Then go lead your family.
