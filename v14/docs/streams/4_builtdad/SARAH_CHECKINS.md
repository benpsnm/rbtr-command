# Built Dad — Partner Check-Ins

One per week. These are for Sarah to initiate if she wants to — not as surveillance, as partnership. The questions are designed to create space Ben might not create himself, because men doing private programmes tend to process internally and forget that their partner sees them better than they see themselves.

These aren't interrogations. They're invitations. Sarah should ask them when they're both calm, kids aren't needing something, and there's a few minutes of real contact available.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "Have you been honest with yourself this week about who you actually are — not who you're working toward?"

*Why this question:* Week 1 is about dropping the performance. Ben is very good at projecting capability. The first week of Built Dad is supposed to crack that open. Sarah sees when it's real and when it's managed. This question gives him permission to be unpolished.

*What to listen for:* Whether he gives a real answer or an optimistic one. If it's optimistic, just say: "I believe you. I just want you to know I see you either way."

---

## Week 2 — The Boys
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "What have you noticed about the boys this week that you might have missed if you weren't paying closer attention?"

*Why this question:* Week 2 is about seeing Hudson and Benson specifically — not as a group, not abstractly, as individual people. This question tests whether the sharper attention is landing.

*What to listen for:* Specificity. If he can tell you something precise about each boy, the week is working. If it's vague, ask a follow-up: "What did Benson actually do that stopped you?"

---

## Week 3 — The Marriage
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "Have you been treating me like I'm part of this, or like I'm just watching it?"

*Why this question:* This is the week Built Dad turns to face the marriage. Ben can go deep on fatherhood and leave Sarah in the background of his own reflection. This question brings her into the frame directly.

*What to listen for:* Whether he answers defensively or openly. It's not a trap — it's a real question. She's asking because she wants to be in it with him, not because she feels excluded. That framing helps.

---

## Week 4 — The Body
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "How is your body actually feeling? Not training metrics — are you taking care of yourself?"

*Why this question:* Ben can mistake punishment for discipline. Week 4 is about physical readiness, which can tip into overtraining or using physical effort to avoid sitting still. Sarah knows the difference between him being well and him being depleted.

*What to listen for:* Whether he distinguishes between tired-in-a-good-way and tired-in-a-depleted-way. If he's not sure, that's the answer.

---

## Week 5 — The Legacy
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "Have you thought about your grandad this week? What came up?"

*Why this question:* This week is the most tender one in the programme. The grandad thread is central to Built Dad — it's the reason there's a template to live up to. Ben doesn't always invite this topic. Sarah asking creates permission to go there without it feeling like therapy.

*What to listen for:* Whatever comes up, let it land. Don't try to fix or frame it. This one might need silence after it.

---

## Week 6 — The Fear
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "Is there anything you're afraid of about this expedition — about us, not just the logistics — that you haven't said out loud yet?"

*Why this question:* Week 6 is the hardest week. It's designed to surface fear. Sarah asking this directly gives Ben a container to speak from that isn't just his own journal. She is the right person to receive this — not because she needs to solve it, because she's his partner and these fears belong to both of them.

*What to listen for:* Be ready for something real. This is not a casual question. If it opens something significant, let the conversation go there. Don't redirect it.

---

## Week 7 — The Commitment
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "Do you feel like I'm in this with you — the expedition, the family, all of it — or do I need to show you that more clearly?"

*Why this question:* Week 7 is about re-choosing. Ben is re-choosing his commitments — Sarah should have the chance to re-affirm hers too, on her own terms. This question invites that without putting the whole emotional load on him to initiate it.

*What to listen for:* His answer will tell you where he is. If he says yes without hesitation, that's good. If there's a pause, or a "mostly," go into that. He may need to hear from her more than he's asked for.

---

## Week 8 — The Hand-Off
**For Sarah to ask:**
> "What's different about you from when this started? Not what you want to say — what you actually feel is different?"

*Why this question:* The last week is about consolidation. Sarah has watched eight weeks of this. She likely sees the shift better than he does. But asking him to name it first means it belongs to him. Then she can reflect back what she's seen.

*What to listen for:* And then tell him what you've seen. Not everything — just the true thing. The thing that would mean most to him to hear confirmed. Week 8 ends on Day 56. This conversation should happen before then, so he carries it into the final day.
