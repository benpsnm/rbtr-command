## Phase 1 · Foundation & Funding (Weeks 1–10)

**Goal:** Lock the plan, money, and workshop. Prove the truck is a viable base. Get tier 1 sponsors confirmed.

**Key Deliverables:**
- Full Arocs strip-down and condition report (engine, chassis, suspension, electrical)
- Workshop lease or arrangement confirmed with access for the full build
- Subframe design brief to fabricator — steel ordered by Week 8
- Goldschmitt suspension spec finalised and order placed by Week 10
- Tier 1 sponsors (9 brands, ~£45,850 value) all approached and at least 4 confirmed

**Sponsor Coordination:**
- Michelin, Victron, Fogstar, EcoFlow — approach Week 1–2, follow up Week 4
- Tata Steel, RED Winches, Clesana, Jaltest, Chesterfield Composites — approach Week 2–3
- Goal: ≥4 Tier 1 confirmations by end Phase 1 (cash or kit agreement in writing)

**Goal:** The truck is structurally transformed. The subframe is welded, suspension upgraded, powertrain sorted.
