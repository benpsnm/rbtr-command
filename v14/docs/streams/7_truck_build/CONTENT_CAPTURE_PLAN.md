# RBTR · Arocs 6×6 · Content Capture Plan
**60 specific filming moments across the 60-week build**

> The build is the hero content for the RBTR channel. Every major milestone is a video.
> Format notes: Most are YouTube longform (8–20 min). Short-form derivatives for Reels/TikTok/Shorts.
> Ben is the presenter. Sarah on camera where relevant. Hudson and Benson cameos where it feels natural.
> Gear: Sony cinema camera (from Week 49 delivery; smartphone until then). DJI drone where specified.

---

## PHASE 1 · Foundation & Funding (Weeks 1–10)

### 1. "We bought a Mercedes Arocs 6×6 — here's why" (Week 1)
- Type: Hero reveal video — most important video of the entire series
- What to film: First shots of the truck. Ben walking around it, hands on chassis, looking up at the cab. Explain: the mission, the family, the truck choice. Why Arocs not Unimog, not Scania, not Land Rover.
- Shot list: Exterior walk-around (all angles). Interior cab. Engine bay. Underside if accessible. Ben talking to camera — emotional, not scripted.
- B-roll: Family reacting to truck. Countryside location if possible.
- Drone: Yes — truck in landscape, wide establishing.
- Thumbnail: Ben in front of Arocs, huge truck, tiny man.

### 2. "The RBTR Truck — full spec breakdown" (Week 1–2)
- Type: Spec deep-dive / companion to reveal
- What to film: Go through every section of the build bible on camera. This is the "what we're building" episode.
- Shot list: Whiteboard or printed plan. Ben talking through each section. Sketch of the layout.

### 3. "Our 60-week countdown — the build plan explained" (Week 2)
- Type: Build plan explainer
- What to film: Gantt chart or timeline on screen. Ben explains Phase 1 through 6. Show the departure date.
- Drone: Optional

### 4. "First look inside — what we're starting with" (Week 2)
- Type: Strip-down / condition video
- What to film: Every fault documented. Rust, worn seals, dodgy wiring. Honest assessment. Don't hide the problems.
- Shot list: Under chassis. Engine bay. Cab interior. Frame rails.
- Tone: Realistic. "This is what expedition trucks actually look like before the build."

### 5. "Workshop tour — where the Arocs will be built" (Week 2–3)
- Type: Behind the scenes / workshop intro
- What to film: The workshop. Introduce the team/fabricator. Show the tools and space.

### 6. "Sponsor announcement #1 — who's backing the build" (Week 6–8)
- Type: Sponsor reveal (first confirmed brands)
- What to film: Ben reveals confirmed sponsors. Show products if received. Explain the relationship.
- Note: Only film this when ≥2 sponsors confirmed in writing.

### 7. "The Goldschmitt decision — why we chose air suspension" (Week 4–5)
- Type: Technical explainer
- What to film: Ben explains air vs coil vs leaf. Show the stock suspension. Render or diagram of Goldschmitt setup.

### 8. "Designing the subframe — with the fabricator" (Week 5)
- Type: Engineering / planning video
- What to film: Meeting with fabricator. Drawings. Explain why the subframe is the skeleton of the build.
- Drone: No

### 9. "Phase 1 wrap — what we learned" (Week 10)
- Type: Phase review
- What to film: Honest assessment of Phase 1. Sponsors confirmed. Plan adjusted. How Ben feels.

## Filming checklist (every major install)

- [ ] Before shot (product unboxed, position marked)
- [ ] Install in progress (hands at work, not posed)
- [ ] After shot (fitted, clean, final)
- [ ] Ben talking to camera: what this does, why it matters, who made it
- [ ] Close-up of brand/product name (for sponsor deliverable)
- [ ] Scale reference (human in frame to show truck size)
