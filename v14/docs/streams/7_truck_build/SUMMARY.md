## The three biggest risks to departure

1. **Goldschmitt lead time** — 10–14 week bespoke part. Order by Week 5. Not Week 6. Not Week 7. Week 5.
2. **Fogstar/Victron cash exposure** — £19,800 in electrical parts due in Phase 2. If neither confirms by Week 10, the financial plan must be reviewed immediately.
3. **Subframe fabrication overrun** — Gate 1 on the critical path. A 4-week slip cascades to a 6–8 week departure delay.

## On realism

This plan is built on honest numbers:
- Ben is the primary builder — solo except where specialist trades are needed
- Phase 4 includes Christmas (weeks 33–34 run light)
- Phase 6 includes a 4-day buffer before the 1 July hard departure date
- Hours estimates account for PSNM commitments and family time — not just build time
- Cost estimates use the mid-range of Build Bible §budget figures
- Sponsor targets are ambitious; 50% hit rate is the financial planning assumption

The plan is achievable. It requires consistent execution from Week 1. The biggest enemy is not the complexity of the build — it's the gap between "I'll sort it this week" and actually sorting it. The Week 1 task list is designed to make that gap impossible to ignore.
