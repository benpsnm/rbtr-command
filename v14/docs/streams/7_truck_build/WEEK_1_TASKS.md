# RBTR · Week 1 Tasks
**What Ben does this week to start the plan officially**
**5–10 May 2026**

This is the week the build goes from plan to action. Every task below has a time budget. Ordered by priority.

---

## Monday 5 May

### 1. Workshop confirmed (2 hours)
**What:** Call or visit the workshop. Confirm access dates, keys, any safety requirements.
If not yet confirmed: this is the single most urgent item. Everything else is blocked without a place to work.
**Output:** Workshop access date confirmed in calendar. Key in hand or date confirmed.

### 2. Create build tracking folder (1 hour)
**What:** Create a shared folder (Google Drive or equivalent) with:
- This plan (Week-by-Week, Phase Breakdown, etc.)
- Sponsor tracker spreadsheet (columns: brand, status, value, contact name, last contact date, notes)
- Parts order tracker (columns: part, supplier, order date, ETA, cost, status)
- Build log template for weekly hours/cost tracking
**Output:** Folder live with all docs in.

### 3. First sponsor emails — Tier 1 batch 1 (2 hours)
**Who:** Michelin, Victron Energy, Fogstar, Chesterfield Composites
**What:** Email each brand. Subject line: "RBTR Arocs 6×6 — Expedition Partnership Opportunity." Keep it short: who we are, what we're building, why them, what we want, link to a sponsor deck or brief summary PDF. Ask for a call.
**Output:** 4 emails sent. Logged in sponsor tracker with date sent.

---

## Tuesday 6 May

### 4. Jaltest diagnostic booking (30 min)
**What:** Contact Jaltest UK (or the nearest certified Jaltest workshop). Book a full HGV diagnostic on the Arocs as early as Week 3–4.
**Output:** Appointment booked. Date in calendar.

### 5. Fabricator outreach (1 hour)
**What:** Contact 2–3 HGV subframe fabricators. Send truck specs and brief. Request site visit + quote for subframe fabrication.
If you already have a fabricator in mind — call them today. Don't email — call.
**Output:** At least one fabricator visit/quote meeting booked for Week 3.

### 6. Filming — Day 1 truck reveal (3 hours)
**What:** Film the reveal video. Smartphone is fine for Week 1. Get:
- Walk-around all four sides of the truck
- Inside the cab
- Engine bay (open the hood)
- Underside (lie on the floor if needed — get the shots)
- Ben talking to camera: the mission, why the Arocs, departure date 1 July 2027
- One wide shot of the truck in a good location (field, open road — not a car park if avoidable)
- Family cameo if Sarah and boys are available
**Output:** 30–60 min of raw footage. Backed up to hard drive and cloud.

---

## Wednesday 7 May

### 7. Second sponsor email batch — Tier 1 batch 2 (2 hours)
**Who:** EcoFlow, RED Winches, Clesana, Jaltest, Tata Steel UK
**Same approach as Monday batch.**
**Output:** 5 more emails sent. Logged.

### 8. Goldschmitt — first contact (30 min)
**Why urgent:** Lead time is 10–14 weeks. This is the longest-lead critical-path part.
**What:** Email Goldschmitt UK or Germany contact (find on their website). Introduce the project. Ask for a spec call to discuss Arocs suspension options.
**Output:** Contact email sent. Follow-up reminder in calendar for 5 days' time.

### 9. Build log — Week 1 entry (30 min)
**What:** Fill in the Week 1 row of the build log: hours worked, costs incurred (£0 so far), mood, what was done. This habit becomes the data for the YouTube update videos later.
**Output:** Week 1 logged.

---

## Thursday 8 May

### 10. Truck condition — document everything (3 hours)
**What:** While at the truck (or plan a specific visit):
- Photograph every visible rust spot
- Check and note all fluid levels
- Check tyre condition and note pressures
- Photograph the chassis from underneath (phone camera, torch)
- Check cab interior: electrics, dash warnings, seat condition
- Note any sounds when started and idling
**Output:** Condition photo folder (minimum 40 photos). Written notes in build log.

### 11. Insurance check (1 hour)
**What:** Confirm the Arocs is currently insured for road use, or arrange traders/fleet cover that covers the build period and test drives.
**Output:** Insurance policy reference. Cover note in hand.

### 12. Workshop lease (if not done Monday) (1 hour)
**What:** If workshop not confirmed on Monday — drop everything and sort this today. Nothing else in this plan can proceed without it.

---

## Friday 9 May

### 13. Fabricator call/meeting (1–2 hours)
**What:** First meeting with fabricator (if booked Tuesday). Bring:
- Truck dimensions and chassis photo
- Brief description of what the subframe needs to achieve
- Any reference photos of similar Arocs builds
Do not discuss full price yet — just establish whether they can do it.
**Output:** Fabricator assessment: yes they can do it / not suited. Book site visit for Week 2–3 if yes.

### 14. Sponsor deck first draft (2 hours)
**What:** One PDF, maximum 5 pages:
1. Cover: RBTR, Ben Greenwood, the Arocs, Departure 1 July 2027
2. The mission: 45 months, 45 countries, family of four
3. The audience: what RBTR is, channel, social presence
4. The build: 25 sections, sponsor opportunities per category
5. The ask: be specific for each brand
**Output:** Sponsor deck draft saved. Will refine next week once condition photos are in.

### 15. End of Week 1 review (30 min)
**What:** Look at the Week 1 checklist. What's done, what isn't.
Update the sponsor tracker. Log hours (target: ~20 this week).
Commit to any postponed items as Week 2 first tasks.
**Output:** Week 1 review logged. Week 2 priorities clear.
