# RBTR · Arocs 6×6 · Sponsor Coordination Timeline
**55 sponsors · ~£142,990 total target value**

> Two types of sponsor relationship:
> **Parts sponsors** — they send physical product. Need confirmed BEFORE we order, as delivery must align with install week.
> **Cash/content sponsors** — they provide money or services. Timing is more flexible but should be locked before the build phase their content covers.
>
> Rule: A parts sponsor must be confirmed (written agreement) at least 8 weeks before their part is needed on-site.
