# RBTR · Arocs 6×6 · Critical Path
**7 milestones that gate departure on 1 July 2027**

Miss any one of these and the departure date moves. These are the non-negotiables.

---

## Milestone 1 · Subframe Complete
**Target: Week 13 (26 Jul 2026)**
**Float: 2 weeks** (Phase 3 box-mount can absorb minor delay — Phase 2 mechanicals cannot)

Why it gates everything:
The subframe is the spine. Suspension, fuel tank, battery box, body mounts — everything physical on this truck is fastened to the subframe. If it's late, every Phase 2 item queues behind it. A 4-week delay here cascades Phase 2 into Phase 3, compresses Phase 4, and may push the departure by 6–8 weeks.

What must happen first:
- Steel spec signed off by fabricator (Week 5)
- Steel ordered from Tata Steel (Week 6)
- Steel delivered (Week 10–11)
- Fabricator workshop slot locked (Week 10)

Early warning signs:
- Fabricator not confirmed by Week 8 → escalate immediately
- Steel delivery later than Week 12 → explore alternative stockholder
- Design changes requested after Week 6 → freeze the spec, absorb in Phase 2 snag

Responsible: Ben + fabricator (TBC)

## Milestone 2 · Habitation Box Mounted
**Target: Week 21 (21 Sep 2026)**
**Float: 1 week**

Why it gates everything:
The box going on is the point of no return — once it's mounted, the interior build timeline is locked. Every Phase 3 and Phase 4 item depends on the box being on, sealed, and accessible from inside. Delay here compresses all interior work.

What must happen first:
- Subframe complete (Milestone 1)
- WS Sherburns fabrication complete
- Chesterfield Composites body panels delivered
- Crane/lift arranged
- All underbody systems (fuel, air lines, chassis wiring) complete before box closes access

Early warning signs:
- WS Sherburns slot not confirmed by Week 13 → alternative body fabricator needed
- Body panels delayed → cannot close the box; affects waterproofing timeline
- Crane booking issues → arrange 3 weeks ahead minimum

Responsible: Ben + WS Sherburns

## Milestone 3 · Electrical First Power-On
**Target: Week 29 (16 Nov 2026)**
**Float: 2 weeks**

Why it gates everything:
The first successful power-on — Victron system live, solar charging, inverter running — proves the electrical architecture works. After this point, all electrical consumers (kitchen, lights, ablutions, comms) are wired in sequence. If the first power-on fails and reveals an architecture problem, weeks 29–35 are at risk.

What must happen first:
- Battery bank installed and BMS configured (Week 18)
- Victron Quattro inverter-charger mounted and wired (Week 18–24)
- Solar panels on roof with MPPT connected (Week 28)
- Shore power hook-up for initial test

Early warning signs:
- Fogstar not confirmed by Week 10 → self-fund or source alternative LFP pack
- Victron not confirmed by Week 10 → same
- Cable sizing errors found → rewire before box is lined (cannot easily reroute through ply)

Responsible: Ben + qualified auto-electrician (24V systems)

## Milestone 4 · Water System Live
**Target: Week 29 (16 Nov 2026) — same gate as electrical**
**Float: 3 weeks** (water can be commissioned up to Week 32 before it blocks Phase 4 kitchen)

Why it gates everything:
Water running through potable lines is required before kitchen and ablutions fit-out. The kitchen sink and shower both depend on confirmed pressure and flow rates. Discovering a leak or low pressure issue after the kitchen is fitted is a Phase 4 nightmare.

What must happen first:
- Tanks installed and fixed (Week 26)
- Pump fitted (Week 27)
- All lines run and pressure-tested (Week 29)
- Berkey filter in-line

Early warning signs:
- Tank custom order late → plumbing cannot start
- Pressure test failure → re-run affected lines before ply goes on

Responsible: Ben + plumber for final pressure test sign-off

## Milestone 5 · Road Test 1 Passed
**Target: Week 53 (3 May 2027) — initial test; snag cleared by Week 54**
**Float: 2 weeks**

Why it gates everything:
Road Test 1 is when everything that doesn't work under load is discovered. Suspension, brakes, electrical, water, heat — all of it behaves differently moving at speed with a full load. Any serious mechanical fault here requires diagnosis, parts, and repair. A 4-week repair window (Weeks 54–57) exists before MOT.

What must happen first:
- All Phase 5 external work complete
- Full load on truck (weighted to approximate departure weight)
- All electrical consumers live
- Water system under test

Early warning signs:
- Phase 5 work running late → compress snag time
- Parts still arriving Week 52 → no-go for full road test
- Suspension fault at Phase 2 not fully resolved → catastrophic at road test

Responsible: Ben (drive) + mechanic witness

## Milestone 6 · MOT / IVA Passed
**Target: Week 55 (17 May 2027)**
**Float: 2 weeks** (can resubmit by Week 57 and still depart)

Why it gates everything:
The truck cannot legally depart the UK without a valid MOT (it's registered as an HGV) and any applicable IVA inspection for modifications. A fail sends it back to the workshop. Two resubmit attempts fit in the window before the hard departure date.

What must happen first:
- Road Test 1 complete and snag list clear
- Lighting, brakes, emissions all compliant
- All modifications documented (subframe, suspension lift, body)
- Pre-MOT check with independent HGV engineer (Week 53)

Early warning signs:
- Suspension lift takes the vehicle outside of the standard MOT envelope → pre-approval needed from DVSA
- Habitation box weight change affects axle loads → check against V5C registered weight
- Electrical modifications require sign-off → get certified engineer to inspect first

Responsible: Ben + HGV MOT station (TBC — book by Week 50)

## Milestone 7 · Customs Carnet Ready
**Target: Week 57 (31 May 2027) — submitted; received by Week 59**
**Float: 1 week** (minimum)

Why it gates everything:
The ATA Carnet de Passages en Douane (or RAC/AA carnet) is the single document that allows the truck to cross into 40+ countries without paying import duty each time. Without it, the expedition stops at the first border. Processing time is 6–8 weeks. If submitted late, Ben is stranded in Europe.

What must happen first:
- Vehicle fully registered and MOT'd (so registration number is confirmed)
- V5C in the correct name and address
- Insurance documents confirming international cover
- List of countries (route plan) submitted with carnet application

Early warning signs:
- MOT late → carnet application cannot start
- Route changes affecting carnet country list → amend before submission, not after
- ATA carnet vs RAC carnet — confirm which body handles each country list

Responsible: Ben + RAC/AA carnet department (apply Week 51, track weekly)
