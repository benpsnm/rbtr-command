### Long Description

There are places you stay, and places that stay with you.

4 Woodhead Mews is not a pretty weekend cottage. It is a considered, high-specification retreat built around one idea: that luxury should feel earned. Exposed materials. Deliberate darkness. A wellness circuit that pushes you and restores you in the same evening. When you leave, you will feel it.

**The House**

The property sits at the end of a private mews in Blacker Hill, a quiet corner of South Yorkshire with none of the noise and all of the access. The exterior gives little away — dark, composed, understated. Step inside and the level of finish becomes immediately apparent.

The interior design leans into darkness deliberately. Deep tones, quality textiles, considered lighting. This is not a showroom; it is a space designed for people who know the difference between something that looks expensive and something that is. Every surface, every fixture, every detail has been chosen with intention.

The kitchen is full-specification. If you cook, you will want to use it. If you don't, the outdoor kitchen on the terrace handles everything from a morning coffee to a full evening spread around the fire pit.

The attic conversion — currently being finished to the same specification as the rest of the property — adds a further dimension to the space: a mezzanine-level retreat that pulls natural light in from above while keeping the same moody, enclosed feeling throughout.

**The Wellness Circuit**

This is what separates 4 Woodhead Mews from any other short-stay property within fifty miles.

*Hot tub.* Full-size, high-jet, properly maintained. The kind you actually want to be in, not the kind you climb out of after six minutes. Best used in the hour before the sauna, or late at night when the sky is clear and there is nothing to do except stay exactly where you are.

*Sauna.* Dry heat, properly hot. Not a glass box in a hotel basement — a dedicated sauna that gets to temperature and holds it. Bring a towel and stay longer than you planned.

*Ice bath.* The part most guests are nervous about. The part most guests say changed something. Cold water immersion at the right temperature, in the right sequence. Go in after the sauna. The contrast is the point.

*Fire pit.* Outside, on the terrace, with seating for six. The fire pit closes the evening. You have been through heat and cold and heat again. Now you sit. The fire does the rest.

*Outdoor kitchen.* The fire pit and the outdoor kitchen are designed to work together. Grill, prep, eat outside. South Yorkshire evenings in summer are underrated. This setup makes the most of them.

**Location**

Blacker Hill puts you fifteen minutes from Barnsley town centre, twenty minutes from the M1, and within easy reach of Sheffield, Rotherham, and the southern edge of the Peak District. It is rural enough to be quiet, connected enough to not feel remote. If you are visiting for an event, a match, or work in the region, this is the right base. If you are visiting for the retreat itself, you have everything you need on-site.

**The Standard**

Linens are hotel-quality. The welcome is genuine. The house is cleaned to a standard that you will notice. The hot tub water is tested and treated before every stay. The sauna is prepared for your arrival. The fire is laid and ready.

If anything is not right, you will have a direct line to the host. Not a bot. Not an automated message. A person.

**Rules and expectations**

This is a private residential property on a mews. Guests are expected to behave accordingly. Noise after 22:00 should be kept to a level that would not wake a neighbour. The wellness circuit is for the use of staying guests only. No events, no parties, no exceptions.

Dogs are welcome on request. Please confirm before booking.

Treat this place with the respect it deserves and it will give you a stay that is hard to replicate anywhere else at any price.

### Long Description

Sometimes a break is not enough. What you need is a reset.

4 Woodhead Mews was built with that in mind. It is a private, high-specification retreat in the Barnsley countryside, designed around a complete wellness circuit — hot tub, sauna, ice bath, fire pit — that does something a hotel spa cannot: it gives you the whole thing, privately, on your own terms, at whatever hour suits you.

**Why contrast therapy works**

The sequence is simple and the effect is profound. You begin in the heat of the sauna — genuine heat, properly hot, not the lukewarm afterthought that most spa hotels offer. Your body relaxes, your muscles release, your mind slows. Then the ice bath: cold water immersion that shocks the system into sharp clarity, floods the body with endorphins, and delivers that feeling — alert, calm, fully present — that people spend years chasing through other means. Back to the hot tub to finish: the warmth after the cold is a different warmth entirely. And finally, the fire pit, outside under the sky, to let everything settle.

Most guests tell us they sleep better that night than they have in months. That is not marketing. That is the circuit working.

**The Space**

The house itself is designed to support rest. Dark interiors, quality materials, deliberate calm. It is not a busy space — no clutter, no noise, no aesthetic anxiety. You arrive, you put your things down, and the environment does the work. The light is right. The textiles are right. The temperature is right.

The kitchen is fully equipped for self-catering at a high level. If cooking is part of how you decompress — properly cooking, not reheating — you have everything you need. The outdoor kitchen on the terrace extends the same into the open air, making the fire pit and the kitchen a natural pair for long evenings.

The attic conversion brings a meditative quality to the upper space: natural light from above, an intimate scale, the same considered finish as everywhere else in the house. A good place to read, to stretch, to do nothing.

**Sleep**

The bedrooms are set up for actual rest. Hotel-quality linens, proper blackout, quiet location — the mews is private and the surrounding area is genuinely still at night. This is not always easy to find. Here, it is the baseline.

**The Location**

Blacker Hill is a quiet village in South Yorkshire, convenient to the regional network but with none of the urban friction. The Peak District's southern reaches are within striking distance for a morning walk. Barnsley, Sheffield, and Rotherham are all under thirty minutes by car. You are accessible without being exposed.

**Who comes here**

Couples who need a proper break from a complicated few months. Small groups of friends who want a wellness weekend without the spa-resort price tag or the shared pool. Solo travellers who need solitude and space. People who have heard about cold water therapy and want to try it properly, not in a gym shower. People celebrating something — an anniversary, a birthday, a milestone — who want the evening to mean something.

They all find something slightly different here. The wellness circuit adapts to how you use it.

**The Host**

Sarah manages the property directly. You will have her contact number. If the hot tub needs attention, if you need a late check-in, if anything is not as it should be, you speak to a person. The property is cleaned, checked, and prepared before every arrival. The towels are fresh. The sauna is on. The hot tub is at temperature.

You have nothing to sort. You only have to arrive.
