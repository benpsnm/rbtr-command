## 4 Woodhead Mews — Platform-Specific Copy

**Note on platform differences:**
- **Airbnb:** Character limits are tight. Guests are deal-hunters and experience-seekers. Copy must hook fast.
- **Booking.com:** More corporate, transactional audience. Cleaner prose, feature-forward.
- **VRBO:** Higher-spend guests, longer stays, family and couple focus. Can afford to be more expansive.
- **Plum Guide:** Curated luxury. Tone must be editorial, never promotional. They may interview Sarah.
- **Direct website:** No algorithm to please. This is the ultimate trust-builder for repeat guests and direct bookers.

### Full Description (VRBO — no hard word limit, use expansively)

**The property that changes how you think about a UK short break.**

Most holiday lets in South Yorkshire offer a house. Woodhead Mews offers something more considered: an entire wellness experience on a private mews, at a price point that would get you a mid-spec hotel room in Sheffield.

**The wellness circuit**

This is the defining feature, and it is set up properly.

The hot tub is outdoor, full-size, heated, and private. You are not sharing it with hotel guests or working to a spa timetable. It is yours for the duration of your stay — morning, noon, or midnight.

The sauna is a dedicated dry-heat structure, not a sauna suite wedged into a bathroom. It reaches temperature properly and holds it. Give it 45 minutes when you arrive and it is ready for the evening.

The ice bath is what most guests are initially uncertain about and most guests rate as the highlight of the stay. Cold water immersion after a proper sauna session produces a response in the body that is difficult to describe and straightforward to enjoy. Try it.

The fire pit closes the evening. Sit around it after the circuit, with a drink, under whatever the South Yorkshire sky offers that night.

The outdoor kitchen works in tandem with the fire pit: grill outside, eat outside, stay outside until you are forced in by the temperature. In summer, that takes longer than you'd expect.

**The interior**

Dark, deliberate, comfortable. This is not a holiday home that has been styled by layering cushions and hanging a farmhouse sign. It is a properly designed interior: considered tones, quality textiles, materials that warrant the rate.

The kitchen is fully specified for people who cook. The bedrooms are set up for proper rest: hotel linen, appropriate light control, genuine quiet. The attic conversion adds a further retreat space — intimate, light from above, the same quality of finish throughout.

**The location**

Blacker Hill is a quiet residential village in South Yorkshire. It is fifteen minutes from Barnsley, twenty from Sheffield, fifteen from the M1 at Junction 36. The Peak District's southern approach — Stanage Edge, the Burbage Valley, Padley Gorge — is within forty minutes.

The mews is private. The surrounding area is genuinely quiet at night. This matters.

**The host**

Sarah owns and manages the property. You will have her number. She answers quickly and in person — not via an automated message system. The property is cleaned and prepared before every stay by a team she trusts, and she checks it herself before significant bookings.

**Who comes here**

Couples who need a proper weekend away, not a hotel break. Groups of friends who want a wellness retreat without the group-booking surcharge at a spa hotel. Solo travellers who want space and access to the wellness circuit on their own terms. People celebrating something — anniversaries, birthdays, milestones — who want an environment that rises to the occasion.

And people who have heard about contrast therapy and want to actually try it. Those guests tend to come back.

## PLUM GUIDE

*Note: Plum Guide does not accept self-submission in the traditional sense. They discover properties through their own research and curation process, then contact the host. This section contains the framing Sarah should use if approached, and the positioning she should build toward publicly so Plum Guide's editorial team finds it.*

### What Plum Guide looks for
Plum Guide is selective. They assess properties against 500+ quality checks covering design, function, and experience. They are looking for the top 1% of holiday lets — not the most expensive, but the most considered.

Woodhead Mews qualifies on:
- Wellness circuit (highly distinctive; very few STRs in Yorkshire offer this combination)
- Design intention (dark luxury is a clear point of view, not a generic holiday let)
- Quality of finish (if the attic conversion completes to the same standard as the rest)
- Owner-managed (Plum Guide strongly prefers direct owner involvement)

### If Plum Guide makes contact — how to respond

Do not oversell. Do not send marketing copy. Send this instead:

> "I'd be delighted to show you the property. It's a privately owned wellness retreat on a small residential mews in South Yorkshire — the main feature is a full contrast therapy circuit: hot tub, sauna, ice bath, and fire pit. The interior is designed with a specific point of view — dark luxury rather than the usual holiday let aesthetic. I manage it myself. When would suit?"
