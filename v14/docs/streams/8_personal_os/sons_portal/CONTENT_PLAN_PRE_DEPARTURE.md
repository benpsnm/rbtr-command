# Sons Portal — Content Plan: Pre-Departure

**Period covered:** Now (May 2026) through 30 June 2027 — approximately 14 months
**Goal:** Keep the portal alive, warm, and growing. A stale countdown is worse than no portal.
**Owner:** Ben (primary), Sarah (Memory Wall)

---

## The Rule Before All Others

Ben writes every word. Every caption. Every voice note.

The portal is not a content machine. It is a record of a dad building something extraordinary for his family. The voice matters more than the production quality. A caption written in 30 seconds at the warehouse on a Friday, covered in dust, is worth more than a polished paragraph written at a desk.

Do not automate. Do not outsource. Do not let it go more than two weeks without something new in it.

## Weekly Cadence

### Every Friday — Truck Progress Photo

**What:** One photo from the warehouse. One caption. Two minutes of Ben's time maximum.

**Where it goes:** Truck Progress Photos section.

**How it works:**
1. Ben takes a photo of whatever happened this week — doesn't need to be visually spectacular
2. Ben uploads it via the admin panel (mobile-friendly upload)
3. Ben writes the caption in the upload field — stream of consciousness, no editing
4. Hits publish. Done.

**What makes a good Friday photo:**
- Something changed visibly from last week
- The scale of the truck is clear (put your hand in the shot, or stand next to it)
- Natural warehouse light is fine. No need for studio shots.
- "Before and after" pairs are gold — if you're halfway through a major install, photograph the mid-point too

**What to do if you miss a Friday:**
Add two the following Friday. Do not skip entirely. Do not add an apology note. Just two photos, slightly further apart in date.

**Caption guidance (see AGE_APPROPRIATE_COPY.md for full detail):**
- Hudson: one or two sentences, a real fact embedded in it
- System uses whichever mode the device is in — but Ben writes one caption and the system applies mode-appropriate display

## Monthly Cadence

### Every Month — Voice Note from the Warehouse

**What:** Ben records a voice note from the warehouse. 2–3 minutes. Speaking directly to the boys. In character — he is their dad at the truck, not a content creator.

**Where it goes:** Memory Wall section.

**How to record:** Phone voice memo. Upload via admin panel. Done. No editing. No re-recording if you stumble — stumbles are real.

**Opening template:**
> "Alright boys, it's [month], I'm at the warehouse, and you won't believe what happened this week…"

**Structure (loose):**
- What's been happening at the warehouse (one thing)
- Something funny or surprising that occurred
- A description of what the truck looks like right now — so they can picture it
- What's coming next
- Close with something they can hold: "Next time you see the truck, the [roof rack / fridge / solar panels] will be in."

**Length:** Aim for 2 minutes. 3 is fine. Under 90 seconds feels short. Over 3 minutes loses a 3-year-old and challenges a 7-year-old.

**Do not:**
- Script it in full — it'll sound scripted
- Re-record more than once
- Wait until you have "something good to say" — the mundane warehouse stuff is what they'll want to hear back in 10 years

### Every Month — One New Packing Item

**What:** One item added to either Hudson's or Benson's packing list.

**Where it goes:** Their Packing List section.

**How it works:**
- Ben or Sarah adds the item via admin panel: name, one-line note, photo
- Alternate between Hudson and Benson (don't add 3 to Hudson and ignore Benson)
- Ideal: photograph the actual item. If it hasn't been bought yet, use a clean product photo as a placeholder.

## Milestone Events

Milestone events are not on a schedule. They happen when the build hits a major moment. They get a different treatment to a regular Friday photo.

### What counts as a milestone

- Roof rack fitted
- Fridge installed and running
- Roof tent up for the first time
- Interior panelling complete
- Solar panels connected
- First test drive after major work
- Truck wrap or paint applied
- Ben sleeps in it for the first time (this is a big one)

### How to handle a milestone

1. **Photo(s):** Take more than one. The milestone gets a small gallery — 2 to 4 photos, not just one.
2. **Caption:** Slightly longer than usual — still kid-language, but Ben is allowed a third sentence for milestone cards.
3. **Optional: short video walkthrough.** Ben walks around or through the truck with his phone, talking to the boys. 60–90 seconds. "Right boys, come with me…" style. Uploaded to the Memory Wall, not the Truck Photos gallery.
4. **Milestone label:** The card is tagged as a milestone in the admin panel. It renders with a slightly different visual treatment — perhaps a banner, perhaps a border colour. Not a trophy. Just a visual distinction.

## Sarah's Role

Sarah is a contributor, not an admin. Her role is specific and bounded.

**What Sarah owns:**
- Memory Wall — drawings and photos
- She has edit access to the Memory Wall section only
- She can upload drawings the boys produce, family photos from the pre-departure period, and (optionally) her own voice note

**What Sarah does not own:**
- Country facts — these are Ben's (he knows the route)
- Truck photos — these are Ben's (she's not at the warehouse)
- Voice notes — these are primarily Ben's (her involvement is optional and welcome, not expected)

**Sarah's upload flow:**
- Simple photo upload via a mobile-friendly form
- Caption field (optional — she can leave it blank and Ben reviews)
- Goes live immediately (no approval required — she has trusted edit access)

**Sarah's voice note (optional):**
If Sarah records a voice note for the boys, it goes in the Memory Wall exactly like Ben's. Same card format. Different name displayed: "From Mum." There is no pressure attached to this. The portal works without it.

## What Not to Do

**Do not automate the content.**
Ben writes every caption. The portal's value is in his specific voice. An AI-generated caption would be obvious and would undermine the whole thing. If Ben is too busy for a caption, the photo waits until he's not.

**Do not gamify.**
No points. No badges. No streaks. No "you've uploaded 10 photos, unlock a reward." This is a family portal, not a productivity app. The boys' engagement metric is whether they ask to look at it — not whether a counter went up.

**Do not let it go stale.**
A countdown with no new photos behind it feels abandoned. The boys will sense it even if they can't articulate it. Two-week maximum between any new piece of content. If life is genuinely difficult and the cadence slips, Ben adds two photos the next Friday and the voice note is slightly longer that month. No formal catch-up plan needed — just fill the gap.

**Do not over-produce.**
Raw is right. The warehouse light, the background noise, the paint on Ben's hands in the photo — all of that is the content. Don't edit it out.

## Content Checklist — Monthly Health Check

At the start of each month, Ben or Sarah runs through this:

- [ ] At least 4 truck photos added in the last 4 weeks
- [ ] At least 1 country fact added this month
- [ ] Voice note recorded (or scheduled for this week)
- [ ] 1 packing item added this month
- [ ] Memory Wall has something new from Sarah in the last 30 days
- [ ] Countdown number is live and correct
- [ ] No broken photo links (quick visual scan)

This takes 5 minutes. It is not a project review. It is a pulse check.
