# Sarah's Portal — Sections

Each section is optional. She uses what she wants and ignores what she doesn't. Nothing is required. Nothing carries over as an obligation.

---

## 1. Mood + Journal

The simplest thing, done gently.

**Mood:** She can log a daily mood using either an emoji set (she picks the set she likes) or a quiet 1–5 scale — whichever feels less like admin. No labels like "good" or "bad." Just a tone.

**Journal:** A single open text field. No prompts appear unless she turns them on. She can write one word or ten paragraphs. There is no word count, no formatting toolbar unless she wants it, no autosave notification interrupting the flow.

**Voice note option:** If she doesn't feel like typing, she can record a voice note instead. It stays unprocessed — no transcription unless she requests it. Just her voice, saved privately.

**Mood history view:** She can see her mood over time as a gentle wave chart — soft colour gradients, no alarming spikes or anomaly callouts. It is not for analysis. It is just a shape of how she's been.

---

## 2. Dreams

Two tabs, different energy.

**Tab 1: Night dreams**
Date, a title she gives it (not auto-generated), free text, and a field for how she felt on waking. No AI interpretation is offered unless she explicitly asks for it. These are hers to make sense of or not.

**Tab 2: Life dreams**
Not goals. Dreams. Things she wants. Places she wants to go. Versions of herself she's curious about. There is no action field, no deadline, no owner. This is a space to want things without immediately being asked how she plans to achieve them.

Both tabs search by keyword and filter by date, but the default view is recent and unhurried.

---

## 3. Friendship Log

The women in her life, held gently.

Each contact has:
- **Name** — first name only in the UI. No last names, no contact details unless she adds them.
- **Last connected** — a date she fills in when they talk. No automatic syncing to her phone.
- **What's going on for her** — a short note about what's happening in her friend's life right now. Just enough to remember where she's at.
- **When Sarah wants to reach out** — not a task, not a calendar entry. Just an intention.

A soft prompt appears — not a notification, a quiet line on the page — when a significant amount of time has passed since she connected with someone. The language is gentle: *"You haven't caught up with [Name] in a while."* It says it once and doesn't repeat it.

There is no social graph, no suggested connections, no link to her email or phone. It is entirely manual because people are not data.

---

## 4. Personal Goals

Hers alone. Not the family goals, not the expedition milestones.

Each goal has three fields:
- **The goal itself** — what she's working toward, in her own words
- **Why it matters to her** — the real reason, not the elevator pitch
- **How she's feeling about it right now** — not a percentage, not a status. A feeling. It can change.

**Pilates qualification** gets its own dedicated space within this section — not just a list entry. It has room for where she is in the course, what she's found hard, what she's proud of, and what's next. It sits with the weight it deserves.

---

## 5. Body

This is not a fitness tracker. It is not a weight log. There are no targets, no deficits, no metrics.

It is a place to notice her body — through pregnancy, through the months after, through the Pilates work she's doing. The fields are open-ended:

- How does my body feel today?
- What did it do?
- What does it need?
- Energy level (a simple low / medium / high — no numbers)
- Aches, tension, anything worth noting
- What felt good in movement today

The pregnancy period is handled without drama. It acknowledges that her body is doing something enormous, without projecting emotions onto it.

**Post-natal section:** After the baby arrives, a gentle return-to-movement log becomes available. It follows her lead — there is no suggested timeline, no "week 6 clearance" prompt unless she adds it herself. She records what she does, how it felt, and nothing else is required.

---

## 6. Reading + Learning

A quiet record of her intellectual life.

**Books:** Three states — reading, want to read, have read. She can add notes, quotes she loved, or nothing at all. The "have read" list is just that — a list, not a review prompt.

**Courses:** The Pilates qualification lives in this section too (as a cross-reference from Goals), with room to log modules completed, things that clicked, questions she wants to ask.

**Things she's learned:** A free-form field she can add to anytime. A quote, a thought, something someone said that stuck. No categories required.

---

## 7. Spaces

The corner of the world she's claiming for herself.

Three sub-sections:

**The room**
Which room in the house is hers. How she wants it to feel. What it needs — furniture, light, quiet, whatever it is. A place to think it through without it becoming a project plan.

**The routine**
What a good morning looks like for her. What a good evening looks like. What she wants to protect, even in a busy season. This is not a habit tracker — she is not logging streaks. It is just a written record of what she's decided matters.

**The time**
Protected hours she has claimed. For example: *Tuesday 7–9pm is mine.* She writes them here so they exist somewhere, stated plainly. These are not synced to a shared calendar unless she chooses. They just live here, acknowledged.
