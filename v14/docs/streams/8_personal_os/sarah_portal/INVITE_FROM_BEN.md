# For Sarah

I built you something. I wanted to explain what it is before you open it, because I think it could easily look like the wrong thing.

It's not a tool so I can see what you're up to. I can't see any of it — I wrote that into the database permissions, not just promised it. Your journal, your mood, your dreams, whatever you put in there — none of it comes to me unless you deliberately send it. I wanted that to be a technical fact, not just something you had to trust me on.

What I actually wanted to give you is a bit of space that's yours. Not ours. Not the family's. Yours.

I know you carry a lot. The pregnancy, the boys, the Pilates course you're working hard for, the friendships you maintain without much fuss, the fact that you're also supposed to be gearing up for a year-long truck expedition while growing a person. I see how much you hold, even when you make it look easy. And I notice that most of the tools we have are about things we're doing together — the house, the kids, the planning. There isn't really anything that just belongs to you.

So I built this.

There's a section for your mood and a journal — no prompts unless you want them. There's a place for the Pilates course, because I wanted that to have a proper home of its own rather than living in a notes app somewhere. There's a friendship log, because I know those relationships matter to you and I thought it might be nice to have somewhere to hold them a bit more intentionally. There's a body section — not a weight tracker, nothing like that, just somewhere to notice how you're feeling physically, which seems relevant given that you are doing something extraordinary right now.

There are other sections too. Ignore what doesn't fit. Use what does. Change your mind about any of it.

I put some journal prompts in there — thirty of them — for the days when a blank page feels like too much. They're not therapy questions. I just tried to write things a friend might ask.

I know this isn't the same as actually giving you a free afternoon or a room of your own or more sleep. I'm working on those too. But this is something I could build, so I built it.

It's yours now. Do whatever you want with it.

Ben
