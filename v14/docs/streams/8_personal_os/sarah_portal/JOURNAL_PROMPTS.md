# Journal Prompts

These are here if free-writing feels like too much of a blank page. They rotate — she doesn't have to go in order. She doesn't have to answer the whole thing. She can write two sentences and stop.

They are not therapy questions. They are just things a friend might ask if she had the time and the nerve.

---

1. What happened in the last week that you haven't really processed yet?

2. What does your body feel like today, honestly? Not "fine." Actually.

3. Is there someone you've been thinking about but haven't called? What's making you wait?

4. What version of yourself are you most curious about right now?

5. What are you looking forward to that isn't about the kids, Ben, or the expedition?

6. What have you let go of recently — by choice or by force — and how do you feel about it?

7. If you had a completely free Saturday with no obligations and no one to consider, what would you actually do?

8. What's something you've been pretending is fine when it isn't quite?

9. How does pregnancy feel in your body right now — not how it's supposed to feel, how it actually feels?

10. Is there something you want that you haven't said out loud yet?

11. Which friendship in your life is giving you the most right now? What is it giving you?

12. Which friendship feels a bit quiet at the moment — and is that OK, or does it need something?

13. What did you learn this week — from the Pilates course, from the kids, from anything?

14. What are you afraid of about the expedition? You're allowed to be afraid of things that are also exciting.

15. What do you imagine a good day looking like in five years? Not grand — just a good, ordinary day.

16. What's something you're good at that you don't think about very often?

17. Is there something you said yes to recently that you wish you'd said no to?

18. How are you sleeping? And what's underneath it if you're not?

19. What does "having space" mean to you right now? Do you have any?

20. What's one thing about being a mother that you find genuinely hard — not hard-but-worth-it, just hard?

21. What's one thing about being a mother that you love in a way you didn't expect?

22. Is there something you want to do before the baby arrives? Something just for you?

23. What part of the Pilates training is surprising you — about the work, or about yourself?

24. What's a place you've been that you still think about?

25. What kind of woman do you want Benson, Hudson, and this new baby to remember?

26. What does Ben not know about how you're doing right now? (You don't have to share this one.)

27. What's something small that made you happy this week that you almost didn't notice?

28. If the expedition goes the way you hope, what does it feel like to be on the other side of it?

29. What part of your life feels most like yours right now?

30. What do you need that no one is currently giving you?
