# Weekly Money Meeting

> Sunday, 9pm. Ben + Sarah. 30 minutes hard stop.  
> This is the one time per week you look at the money together. Take it seriously.

---

## Pre-Meeting Setup

Have the following open **before** 9pm — not during:

- [ ] Daily P&L for the week (each entity)
- [ ] Bank balances for all accounts (screenshots or live)
- [ ] Outstanding invoices list (pulled from Supabase, >7 days old)
- [ ] Any large transactions you flagged during the week
- [ ] The decisions log from last week — check what was committed and whether it happened

If the data isn't ready by 8:55pm, one of you gets it while the other makes tea. The meeting starts on time.

## Close

When the 30 minutes is done, close the laptops. The lid goes on.

No "just one more thing" after the meeting closes. If it matters, it goes on the list for next Sunday. If it's genuinely urgent, it wasn't a money meeting issue — handle it separately.

> The point of this meeting is not to solve everything. It's to know where you stand.
